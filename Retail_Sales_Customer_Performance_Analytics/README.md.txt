# Retail Sales & Customer Performance Analytics

## Objective
Analyze retail sales data to understand revenue, customer, product, category, city and monthly performance.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- Power BI
- DAX

## Dataset
- Customers
- Products
- Sales

## Dashboard Pages
1. Executive Overview
2. Customer Analysis
3. Product Analysis
4. City Analysis
5. Trend Analysis

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
